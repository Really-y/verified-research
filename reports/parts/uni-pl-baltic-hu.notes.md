## 0. Country-level rules

### Poland (PL)
- **Attestat access / recognition** — VERIFIED: since 1 July 2025 NAWA (not the kurator) issues the "individual recognition
  statement" for school certificates. Statement is *required* for certificates from outside EU/OECD/EFTA and outside bilateral
  agreements, so it applies to an Azerbaijani attestat. Free of charge, PDF, via SYRENA. Certificates already recognised by a
  Kurator Oświaty are accepted. Universities additionally ask for an apostille/legalisation (e.g. Białystok UT lists "Apostil or
  legalization of High Secondary School Certificate").
  Whether a sub-bachelor (college) diploma counts as a secondary certificate for NAWA: Not verified (NAWA decides case by case;
  page says lists are "not exhaustive").
- **Distance learning (the brief's "≤50%" assumption is only half right)** — VERIFIED, Rozporządzenie MNiSW w sprawie studiów,
  consolidated text Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1207, § 13: "1) 50 % liczby punktów ECTS, o której mowa w § 3 ust. 1 pkt 1 – w przypadku
  studiów o profilu praktycznym; 2) 75 % liczby punktów ECTS, o której mowa w § 3 ust. 1 pkt 1 – w przypadku studiów o profilu
  ogólnoakademickim." § 12 ust. 2: practical-skills classes may use distance methods only "pomocniczo". § 12 ust. 1 pkt 4:
  students must be able to have personal consultations "w siedzibie uczelni lub w jej filii". The cap is the legal ceiling for
  the *programme*; a university is not obliged to use it — the actual share depends on each programme's study plan (Not verified
  for any specific programme). Exams/on-site verification: Not verified per university.
- **Tuition in Polish-language programmes** — VERIFIED (art. 324 ust. 2 PSWN, Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1571): fee-free only for listed
  groups (EU/EEA, permanent residents, refugees, Karta Polaka, spouses of Poles, **holders of a Polish-language certificate at
  level C1** etc.). An ordinary Azerbaijani applicant pays; with an official C1 Polish certificate (art. 11a ustawy o języku
  polskim) he would study in Polish free of tuition. WUT's decision 131/2026 confirms foreigners pay Polish-language fees unless
  in art. 324(2).
- **Attendance** — VERIFIED example (AGH Study Regulations, in force 1 Oct 2023): participation compulsory in all classes except
  lectures; teacher may make lectures compulsory too; absences must be justified within 7 days.

### Hungary (HU)
- **Stipendium Hungaricum** — VERIFIED: Azerbaijan–Hungary MoU on SH for **2024–2026**, "planned to allocate 200 annual
  scholarships" to Azerbaijani citizens (edu.gov.az), corroborated by Hungarian Embassy Baku document. SH = "Tuition-free
  education – exemption from the payment of tuition fee" + HUF 43,700/month stipend + dorm/accommodation contribution.
  Latest published deadline 2026-01-15 (2026/27 cycle); nomination by the sending country required (Azerbaijan: Ministry /
  State Agency for Science and Higher Education). 2027/28 call and MoU renewal beyond 2026: Not verified (ASSUMPTION: deadline
  mid-January 2027). Admission exams by universities March–May.
- Educational Authority (Oktatási Hivatal) recognition of attestat: Not verified; universities assess certificates themselves.

### Czechia (CZ)
- **Free in Czech** — VERIFIED: studyin.gov.cz (DZS/Ministry) "Degree programmes taught in Czech at public universities in Czechia
  are free of charge, regardless of your nationality." Act 111/1998 § 58(4): fee for study is set (mandatory) only where the
  programme is taught in a foreign language; § 58(1) admission fee max 20 % of base; § 58(3) fee for over-long study.
- Czech level: "Most programmes taught in Czech require B2 level Czech" (VERIFIED).
- Preparatory Czech route: the official portal itself lists "the cost of a preparatory Czech course" as a cost the student
  covers → no verified fee-free preparatory route. Exact prices of CTU/Charles Univ. prep courses: Not verified.
- Recognition: nostrification (krajský úřad) or university verification (CTU Rectorate) — VERIFIED for CTU.
