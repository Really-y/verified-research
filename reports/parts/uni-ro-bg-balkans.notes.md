# uni-ro-bg-balkans — notes (RO, BG, RS, MK, BA; ME/AL not covered)

Accessed 2026-11-27. "VERIFIED (F#n)" = finding index n (0-based) in `uni-ro-bg-balkans.findings.json`; all 34 quotes were checked against the live source with the plugin's `verify_report` functions (all OK). Fees are as published (EUR); no currency conversion done here.
Budget reference: USD 800–2,000/yr. The orchestrator converts. As a rough guide, anything above ~1,800–1,850 EUR is likely over USD 2,000.

## Country-level rules

**Romania (RO)**
- Admission of non-EU citizens is based on the Ministry's "scrisoare de acceptare la studii" (letter of acceptance), defined in OME 5552/2024 (Monitorul Oficial 742/30.VII.2024). VERIFIED (F#0).
- Non-EU fee-payers pay in foreign currency ("cu plata taxelor de școlarizare în valută"). VERIFIED (F#1).
- For a foreign-language programme, the student needs a language certificate "conform solicitării instituției de învățământ superior". That means each university sets the level, and there is no national IELTS minimum. VERIFIED (F#2).
- The national minimum fee act is Government Ordinance (OG) 22/2009 (approved by Law 1/2010; 270 EUR/month for technical fields according to secondary search snippets). **Not verified:** legislatie.just.ro refused the connection, and no other official copy could be fetched. ASSUMPTION/inference: UPB's prep-year fee of 2430 EUR (F#5) equals 9 × 270. This is consistent with the 270 EUR/month minimum but is not a quote of the act.
- An Azerbaijani "Tam Orta Tahsil Haqqinda Attestat" is on the list of accepted diplomas annexed to the Univ. Craiova 2026 procedure. VERIFIED (F#9).
- Attendance and online share for full-time study: **Not verified** (no legal text retrieved).

**Bulgaria (BG)**
- Fees for third-country students are set per university by a Council of Ministers decision under Art. 95(8) of the Higher Education Act (ZVO). VERIFIED from the **draft** 2026/2027 decision on strategy.bg (F#11). The final adopted text was not retrieved.
- In the draft, fees for third-country students at public universities are mostly 2,700–4,300 EUR for bachelor's programmes, for example Sofia University Informatics at 4 020 EUR (F#10). They are generally above budget.
- Recognition of secondary diplomas (RAICIHE / Regional Education Office) and NEAA accreditation: **Not verified** in the time available.

**North Macedonia (MK)**
- A foreign secondary diploma must be nostrified by the Ministry of Education and Science before enrolment. VERIFIED (F#19, FINKI page; UKIM page F#18 says it takes "several weeks").
- Accreditation is by the Board for Accreditation of Higher Education. **Not verified:** the registry was not checked for IBU, IVU or SEEU.

**Bosnia & Herzegovina (BA)** — HEA BiH / cantonal accreditation status: **Not verified** (IUS lists "HEA Accreditation" in its site menu, but this is not a registry check).

**Serbia (RS)** — NAT accreditation: **Not verified**.

## Universities

### 1. Ss. Cyril and Methodius University (UKIM), Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FINKI), Skopje, North Macedonia — BEST BUDGET CANDIDATE (borderline)
- Accreditation: public university; registry check **Not verified**.
- Programme: "Software Engineering and Information Systems in English" (6 or 8 semesters), 120 international places. VERIFIED (F#13). Other options are Computer Science, taught in Macedonian (F#14), and Political Studies (in English) at the Faculty of Law (F#15).
- Tuition for international students, 2026/2027: **2300 EUR per 60 ECTS** for SEIS in English (F#13). Computer Science (Macedonian) is 2000 EUR (F#14), Political Studies in English 2000 EUR (F#15), and Economics (Macedonian-taught) 1500 EUR (F#16). Source: the official UKIM Call 2026/27. Note: 2026/27 values; the 2027/28 call is not yet published.
- Other fees: 750 MKD IKSA student-activities fee (UKIM page, quoted in notes only and not a separate finding). Nostrification costs: **Not verified**.
- Admission: secondary diploma with apostille plus nostrification decision from the Ministry of Education and Science (F#19). Whether the Azerbaijani attestat is accepted is ASSUMPTION: likely accepted via nostrification, not verified specifically. Sub-bachelor diploma: **Not verified**. The Call says selection is by "relevance of the previously completed secondary education" and GPA.
- Minimum grades: **Not verified** (no minimum GPA found for foreigners).
- English: "Proof of English language proficiency, if required by the study program" (FINKI page). UKIM runs a one-year "English Language for Foreign Students" programme for foreigners in English programmes. VERIFIED (F#17). No IELTS minimum found: **Not verified** whether B1 is enough.
- Attendance and online elements: **Not verified**.
- Application: UKIM electronic application plus documents in person; the foreign-student window runs "29 August to 30 September". VERIFIED (F#18). Originals must be submitted **in person**.
- Verdict: 2300 EUR is slightly above USD 2,000 at current rates. The Macedonian-taught options (1500–2000 EUR) need Macedonian language. Borderline fit.

### 2. South East European University (SEEU), Tetovo/Skopje, North Macedonia (public-private not-for-profit)
- Accreditation: **Not verified** in the registry.
- Programmes: the Faculty of Contemporary Sciences and Technologies offers Computer Sciences and others, and there is a Faculty of Business and Economics. The English-language option exists (FAQ refers to "study programmes in the English language"). Exact CS-in-English programme list: **Not verified**.
- Tuition 2026/2027 intake: **2.400 EUR/yr** for CST and also for Business and Economics. VERIFIED (F#24). It is **Not verified** whether foreigners pay a different rate; the authoritative Call PDF is on Google Drive and could not be text-extracted.
- Merit scholarships are 60% (GPA 5.00) or 40% (GPA 4.50–4.99) of tuition (same page). An Azerbaijani 4/5 average would not qualify, and it is **Not verified** that these scholarships are open to foreigners.
- Other fees: application fee "for payment from abroad, 25 Euros". VERIFIED (F#26).
- English: **B1 (CEFR) by certificate OR the SEEU Language Centre institutional test**. VERIFIED (F#25). This fits the student's B1 level and lack of IELTS.
- Attendance and online elements: **Not verified**.
- Deadlines: set in the annual Call (Google Drive). **Not verified**.
- Verdict: closest English-taught fit on language (B1 internal test), but 2,400 EUR is above budget unless discounted.

### 3. International Balkan University (IBU), Skopje, North Macedonia (private, Turkish-founded)
- Accreditation: footnotes in the Call show programmes in "reaccreditation". Registry: **Not verified**.
- Tuition for international students in the **2025/2026** Call (the 2026/27 call was not found): Computer Engineering **6000 EUR** (F#20); International Economics / E-Business 4000 EUR (F#21). Balkan citizens pay less: 4000 EUR for Computer Engineering and 2000 EUR for Economics, but an Azerbaijani citizen is "International".
- Other fees: 500 EUR for nostrification and residence-permit services (F#23). English preparatory year: 5000 EUR for internationals (same PDF).
- English: **internal exam for all; below 60/100 means a one-year preparatory English programme**. VERIFIED (F#22).
- Deadlines 2025/26: enrolment windows 01.07–31.08.2025, 02.09–30.09.2025 and 13.01–14.02.2026 (same PDF). The 2027 dates are **Not verified**.
- Attendance and online elements: **Not verified**.
- Verdict: **over budget** (4,000–6,000 EUR for internationals).

- Verdict: **over budget** (2,200–2,600 EUR, plus a mandatory language test and enrolment fee).
